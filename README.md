# Turkana Civic Justice Website

Official website for Turkana Civic Justice (TCJ) - A Community-Based Organization promoting democracy, good governance, human rights, and peace in Turkana County.

## 🌟 About TCJ

Turkana Civic Justice works towards:
- Democratic values and civic education
- Youth and women political participation  
- Human rights protection and promotion
- Peaceful coexistence through dialogue
- Transparency and accountability in leadership
